How to read this

Pan for 5 seconds, then revisit the same area. Compare again after changing a setting. P95 means 95% of samples took this time or less. A 60 fps frame has about 16.7 ms.

Main-thread drawing measures synchronous render submission. GPU timing is sampled when supported; it excludes screen presentation. Delivery includes fetching, cache access and regional processing. Image preparation covers the instrumented composition path, not every worker. Long-task blocking counts time beyond 50 ms per task. These are clues, not total CPU/GPU usage or a guaranteed cause. Hidden tabs pause sampling.
