Data credits

Bushline Maps is built on open data from New Zealand's public agencies, and on open-source software. Thank you to everyone who makes it available.

Maps and imagery

  • Toitū Te Whenua Land Information New Zealand (LINZ) – aerial imagery, NZ Topo50 maps, topographic vector data, elevation and the New Zealand Gazetteer of place names, via LINZ Basemaps. Licensed under CC BY 4.0. Aerial imagery is supplied to LINZ by its imagery providers.
  • Overview imagery (the zoomed-out view of the whole country) is averaged by Bushline Maps from LINZ's aerial imagery, with the coastline blended using Natural Earth data (public domain). A few small areas on the West Coast where LINZ has no imagery use Esri World Imagery (© Esri and its data providers).

Walks, trails, huts and hunting areas

  • Department of Conservation Te Papa Atawhai (DOC) – walking and tramping experiences and their lines, tracks, huts and recreational hunting permit areas, from DOC's open data. Licensed under CC BY 4.0. Walk descriptions and photos link to DOC's website. Bushline Maps is not affiliated with or endorsed by DOC.
  • Te Araroa Trust – the Te Araroa trail and its kilometre markers, from the Trust's official GPS files at teararoa.org.nz. Licensed under CC BY 4.0. Bushline Maps is not affiliated with or endorsed by Te Araroa Trust.

Software

  • MapLibre GL JS – BSD 3-Clause licence.
  • Open Sans – SIL Open Font Licence (map labels, via LINZ Basemaps).
