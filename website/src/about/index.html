Questions

Is the map really free?

Yes. The map, walks, huts, hunting areas and route planning are free on the web, with no account. The Android app adds offline maps and navigation for use on the hill.

Where does the data come from?

Aerial imagery, topographic maps and elevation from Toitū Te Whenua Land Information New Zealand (LINZ). Walks, tracks, huts and hunting permit areas from the Department of Conservation (DOC). Both under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. See data credits.

Is it an official DOC app?

No. Bushline Maps is independent. It uses DOC's open data and links to DOC's own pages – always check DOC's alerts and track conditions before you go.

Can I rely on it for navigation?

Use it alongside, not instead of, a paper map, compass and personal locator beacon. Maps can be out of date, phones run flat, and conditions change. Tell someone your plans.

Do you track me?

No. The website has no accounts, no cookies and no analytics; your routes stay in your browser. See the privacy policy.
