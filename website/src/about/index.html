Questions

Is the map really free?

Yes. The map, walks, huts, Te Araroa and route planning are free on the web, with no account. The app adds offline maps for use on the hill.

Where does the data come from?

New Zealand's public agencies: imagery, topographic maps and elevation from Toitū Te Whenua Land Information New Zealand; walks, huts and hunting areas from the Department of Conservation; Te Araroa from the Te Araroa Trust. See data credits.

Can I rely on it for navigation?

Use it alongside a paper map, compass and personal locator beacon, not instead of them. Check DOC alerts and conditions before you go, and tell someone your plans.

Support

Found a problem, a track that's drawn wrong, or have an idea? Email {{site.email}} – we read everything. For a problem with the map, include the place (or copy the map's web address) and what you expected to see.

Using the map

How do I tilt and turn the map?

On a computer, hold the right mouse button (or Ctrl) and drag. On a phone or tablet, drag up or down with two fingers to tilt, and twist two fingers to turn. The 2D/3D button switches between a flat and a tilted view.

How do I see a walk's details?

Tap a track or its name. Walks show DOC's description, distance, ascent and descent, an elevation profile and the huts along the way. Drag across the profile to measure a section.

How do I plan a route?

Open the menu and choose New route, then tap the map. Tap near a track and the route follows the tracks to that point; tap anywhere else and it goes straight. Tap a hut to lock onto it. Drag a point to move it – near a track it slides along it. Your routes are saved in this browser.

Can I get my route onto my phone?

Use Share on the route to copy its link, and open the link on your phone. In the Android app you can save it and take the area offline.

Why does a walk look different from DOC's map?

Walks are drawn from DOC's own line data, joined into one route and starting where DOC's line starts. Side trips are left out of the distance. If something looks wrong, please email us the walk's name.

Safety

Razorback Maps is a planning tool. Always carry a paper map, compass and personal locator beacon, check DOC's alerts and the weather before you go, and tell someone your plans. Visit AdventureSmart for trip planning advice.

Privacy policy

Last updated {{site.updated}}

Razorback Maps is made by {{site.legalName}} ("we") in Aotearoa New Zealand. This policy covers the website at {{site.domain}} and the Razorback Maps Android app. We handle personal information in line with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

In short: there are no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. Your routes and your location stay on your device. If you ask us to tell you when the app is ready, we keep your email address for that alone. To show the map, your device fetches map data directly from the data providers listed below.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. We don't ask for your name or email, set tracking cookies, or use analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply, and delete them when they're no longer needed.

"Get notified" sign-ups

If you enter your email address to hear when the app is ready, we keep that address, which phone you chose (Android or iPhone) and when you signed up. We use it only to email you about the app's release or its test version, at most a few times, and never share or sell it. It is stored with Cloudflare (our host). We delete it once the app you asked about is out, or sooner if you ask: email {{site.email}}.

Information kept on your device

  • Routes you plan are saved in your browser's local storage (website) or in the app's private storage (app). They never leave your device unless you share a route link yourself.
  • Map settings such as layers and the last map position are kept on your device the same way.
  • Offline regions you download in the app are stored in the app's storage on your phone.

Clearing your browser's site data, or uninstalling the app, removes all of this.

Your location

The app asks for permission to use your location to show your position on the map. Your location is used only on your phone, is never sent to us or anyone else, and is not stored. The website shows your location only if you press the location button and your browser asks you to allow it; it too stays in your browser.

Map data providers

To draw the map, your browser or phone requests map tiles and data directly from these services. Like any website, they receive your IP address and the area of the map being viewed, under their own privacy policies:

  • Toitū Te Whenua Land Information New Zealand (LINZ) Basemaps – aerial imagery, topographic maps and elevation.
  • Department of Conservation (DOC) – walks, tracks, huts and hunting areas, through DOC's ArcGIS data services hosted by Esri, and DOC's website for photos and walk pages (the app reads DOC's walk pages directly).
  • Esri World Imagery – the Satellite map, and a few small areas of the West Coast where LINZ has no imagery.
  • Cloudflare – hosts this website and our overview imagery. Cloudflare processes request logs (including IP addresses) to deliver and protect the service.

We don't receive or keep these requests.

Sharing a route

A route share link contains the route itself (its points and name). Anyone you send it to can see that route. We don't store shared routes.

Children

Razorback Maps isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.
